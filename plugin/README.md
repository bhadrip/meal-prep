# Meal Prep

Meal Prep combines a reusable planning skill with a remote MCP connector. ChatGPT loads household context, dietary restrictions, preferred stores, weekly schedules, retrospectives, graph-linked meal experiences, household memories, saved recipes, and pantry data before coordinating breakfasts, lunches, dinners, snacks, prep, and shopping. Feedback connects the recipe or variant, the actual meal occurrence, its week, canonical themes and audiences, and an actionable next-time adjustment. Repeated patterns become household preferences only after review. The remote service persists approved meal plans and shopping lists in Supabase and can render MCP-served household onboarding, a compact household snapshot, an interactive weekly calendar, or a store-grouped checklist directly in chat. Saving a list never places an order.

After installing the plugin, open its **Connectors** tab and connect the Meal Prep service. Authentication is handled by the service's OAuth flow.
